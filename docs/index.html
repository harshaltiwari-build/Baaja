FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Baaja free?

Yes. Baaja is free, ad-free and open source.

Do I need to sign in?

No. You can sign in with Google to use your YouTube Music library, or continue as a guest.

Which build should I download?

Most people want Baaja.apk. Pick the Google Cast build if you cast to a Chromecast or similar device.

Does it work in my region?

Baaja works wherever YouTube Music is available in your region.

Who makes Baaja?

Baaja is designed and built by Harshal Tiwari, on top of the open-source Metrolist, InnerTune and OuterTune projects.

Is Baaja affiliated with YouTube or Google?

No. Baaja is an independent project and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
