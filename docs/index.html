बाजा बजाओ.
Music for every mood.

Baaja is a free, ad-free and open-source YouTube Music client for Android, with a bold light and dark design. Designed and built by Harshal Tiwari.

Download Baaja See features

Android 8.0 or newer · Free forever · GPL-3.0

What you get

Your YouTube Music

Sign in with Google to bring likes, playlists and subscriptions with you, or just continue as a guest.

Bold light and dark design

Ink outlines, offset shadows and a purple accent, with a matching dark mode.

Speed dial

Your favourite playlists a tap away, plus a "Surprise me" card.

Lyrics

Synced lyrics that follow the song, with a share card.

Listen together

Start a room, share the code, and everyone hears the same song in sync.

Import from YouTube

Copy any YouTube Music playlist into your Library by link.

Sound

Equalizer with quick tone, normalization, skip silence and transitions.

Updates inside the app

Baaja tells you when a new version is out and installs it in one tap.
