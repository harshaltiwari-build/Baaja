Questions

Good to know.

Is Baaja really free?

Yes. It is free software under the GNU GPL v3, with no ads and no tracking of you by Baaja. The source code is public.

Do I need to sign in?

No. You can play music as a guest. Signing in with Google brings your YouTube Music library, and it happens on Google's own page, so Baaja never sees your password.

Which build should I download?

Most people want Baaja.apk. Pick "with Google Cast" if you cast to a Chromecast or similar device.

Does it work where I live?

Baaja needs YouTube Music to be available in your region. If it isn't, a VPN or proxy through a supported region is required.

Who makes Baaja?

Baaja is designed and built by Harshal Tiwari, on top of the open-source Metrolist, InnerTune and OuterTune projects. Thank you to everyone behind them.

No. Baaja is not affiliated with, endorsed by or associated with YouTube, Google LLC or Metrolist Group LLC. All trademarks belong to their owners.
