Baaja is a bold, open-source music player for Android that streams from YouTube Music.

Features:

- Play any song or video from YT Music, with background playback
- Personalised quick picks and a full library: songs, albums, artists and playlists
- Search for songs, albums, artists, videos and playlists
- Sign in to YouTube Music and sync your library
- Live lyrics with a lyric preview
- Sound shaping: equalizer, tempo and pitch, skip silence, audio normalisation
- Quality settings for streaming and downloads
- Smart downloads and offline caching
- Automix and queue management
- Audio output picker
- Local music from your device
- Spotify playlist import
- Listen Together with friends
- Identify music playing nearby
- SponsorBlock support
- Automatic backups
- Editable profile
- Sleep timer
- Light, dark and black themes
